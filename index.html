📖 System Guide & Smart Rota Rules

⚡ Smart Rota & Role Sequence Rules

1. SIC Priority Allotment: Sabse pehle SIC (Role 3: `M3, E3, N3`) allot hota hai.
2. Single Role Priority: Agar single-role employee (jaise role `2`) shift me hai, to use uska role diya jata hai aur dual-role wale ko role `1` assign hota hai.
3. Dual Role Sequence (`E1-E2-M1-M2-N1-N2-O`): Multi-role employees shift pair ke 1st day par role `1` aur 2nd day par role `2` handle karte hain.
4. Weekly OFF: Single weekly off rule ke tehat hafte me exactly 1 Off milta hai.
